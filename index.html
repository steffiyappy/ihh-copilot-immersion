About this workshop

A practical, executive-level immersion designed specifically for IHH Healthcare. We move from strategic context → live demos → hands-on exercises.

What you will leave with

  • Experience using Researcher (Critique + Model Council) for multi-model market intelligence
  • A working ESG dashboard built with Copilot in Excel, and a draft CR report section in Word
  • Your own custom AI agent: Brand Governance, Finance Policy Advisor, or Sustainability Guide

What this workshop is not

  • It is not a feature walkthrough: every tool is demonstrated in an IHH-specific context

Tools Used TodayM365 Copilot surfaces

🔍
Researcher (Critique)

The default Researcher mode: keep model selector on Auto. One model researches and drafts; a second reviews source reliability, completeness, and evidence grounding before producing one polished report. Outperforms single-model by +13.88% on the DRACO benchmark.

⚖️
Model Council

Select Model Council in the Researcher model picker. Multiple AI models each run your prompt independently and produce full standalone reports. A third judge model then writes a cover letter: highlighting agreements, divergences, and unique insights from each model.

📊
Excel Copilot

Natural-language data analysis, dashboard creation, trend charts, and RAG status tables: no formulas needed.

📝
Word Copilot

Document drafting: strategic memos, CR reports, board briefings: grounded on your uploaded data files.

🎨
PowerPoint Copilot

Presentation creation and storyline improvement: strategy decks, ESG investor updates, QBR presentations.

📈
Analyst Agent

Out-of-the-box AI analyst built into M365 Copilot. Drop in a dataset and ask complex questions: valuation models, critical path analysis, peer benchmarking. No setup required.

🤖
Agent Builder

Build a custom AI agent in 15 minutes, grounded on your SharePoint documents. Finance Advisor, Brand Agent, or Sustainability Guide.

👥
Cowork

Delegate multi-step tasks to Copilot. Describe the outcome: "analyse ESG data, draft email, schedule meeting": it creates a plan and checks in before taking actions.

Data privacy reminder: Do not paste real patient data, employee identifiable information, or commercially sensitive IHH data into Copilot during this session. Use the sample files provided. Exercise 1 · Strategic Intelligence15 min · Researcher + Model Council

When to do this: Session 3 starts at 10:00. Exercise 1 runs 10:00–10:15. Open M365 Copilot at copilot.microsoft.com and click Researcher.

Use Researcher's Critique mode (dual-model: generate + review) for deep competitive intelligence on Malaysia's private healthcare market, then use Model Council to see a multi-model view side-by-side.

Task 1.15 min🔍 Researcher · Auto (Critique)

Research Malaysia private healthcare market

In M365 Copilot, open Researcher. Keep the model selector on Auto: this is Critique, the default dual-model mode. One model researches and drafts; a second independently reviews source reliability, completeness, and evidence grounding before the final report is produced. Use this prompt:

Research Malaysia private healthcare market: analyse IHH Healthcare, Columbia Asia, KPJ Healthcare, Sunway Medical. Compare market share, bed capacity, strategic focus, recent M&A activity, digital health initiatives, and medical tourism positioning. Include growth projections for 2026–2028. Cite all sources.
The report you receive has already been reviewed and strengthened by a second model. Check the citations: Critique enforces strict evidence grounding, requiring every key claim to be anchored to a reliable, citable source before the final output.
Task 1.25 min⚖️ Model Council

Side-by-side: multi-model view on strategic opportunities

In Researcher, click the model picker and switch to Model Council. Both models will run your prompt at the same time: each producing a complete, standalone report. Use this prompt:

What are the top 3 strategic opportunities for a private hospital group in Malaysia over the next 3 years? Consider: ageing population, medical tourism from Indonesia and the Middle East, digital health penetration, insurance market growth, and the regulatory environment. Provide specific, evidence-based recommendations.
Read the cover letter: written by a dedicated judge model that compares both reports and highlights where the models agree, where they diverge (in framing, magnitude, or interpretation), and the unique insight each model brings. That’s the real value of Council.
Task 1.35 min📝 Word Copilot

Turn research into a strategic memo

First, get your Task 1.1 research into Word: the method depends on which mode you used:

If you used Critique (Auto): In your Researcher report, click the Export button → Convert to Word. The document will be saved automatically to your OneDrive. Open it from OneDrive.
If you used Model Council: Choose the report you prefer. Select all → Copy. Open a blank Word document and paste. Save it to your OneDrive.

Now open the Word document. In the Copilot pane, use this prompt to draft the board memo:

Based on the research content in this document, draft a 1-page executive memo for the IHH Healthcare Strategy Committee. Structure: - Market overview: Malaysia private healthcare market size, growth rate, key dynamics - Competitive landscape: IHH's position vs KPJ, Columbia Asia, Sunway Medical - 3 strategic priorities for FY2026–2027 with rationale - 90-day immediate actions (3 bullets) Format: Board-ready, professional, concise. Use [PLACEHOLDER] for any data point not confirmed in the research. Maximum 400 words.

📥 Referral Triage Queue · Outlook, Excel, Teams · Moderate

Processes the inbound referral inbox: extracting referring provider, reason, urgency, and required documentation: into a ranked triage worklist so intake staff work the highest-priority cases first.

Process all referral emails I've received in the last 7 days. For each referral email: 1. Classify urgency as Urgent / Routine / Information-only based on referral language and clinical indicators. 3. Flag any referral missing required documentation with a specific list of what's needed. 🔫 Surgical Case Report & CPD Presentation · OneDrive, Outlook, Teams, Word, PowerPoint · Advanced

Assembles all clinical documentation for a complex or unique surgical case into a structured CPD case presentation and a publication-ready case report draft. Suitable for laparoscopic liver resection, pancreaticogastrostomy, and other technically demanding procedures worth documenting for peer learning and journals.

⚠️ Tenant note: Steps referencing Outlook emails and Teams channels only work on your own corporate tenant, not the immersion demo accounts.

I performed a laparoscopic left lateral sectionectomy (liver resection, Segments 2 and 3) on 15 April 2025 at Contoso Central Hospital for a 58-year-old patient with a 4.2 cm hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC). I want to submit this case for CPD credit and write it up as a case report for publication. Step 1 - Assemble the case record: 1. Find the operative note, anaesthesia report, and intraoperative imaging for this case in my OneDrive files from April 2025. 2. Search Outlook for pre-op MDT discussion emails, post-op correspondence with the oncology team, and pathology or histology reports referencing case identifier LLR-APR2025-01. 3. Pull the 30-day post-op follow-up notes and any complication records from my recent files. 4. Search the Hepatobiliary Surgery Teams channel for any case discussion threads about this patient. Step 2 - Build the CPD presentation: Create a PowerPoint titled "Laparoscopic Left Lateral Sectionectomy - LLR-APR2025-01 - CPD Presentation" with these slides: - Slide 1: Case overview (patient profile, presentation, imaging findings - anonymised) - Slide 2: Pre-operative planning and MDT decision rationale - Slide 3: Operative technique - key steps and intraoperative challenges encountered - Slide 4: Intraoperative findings and real-time decisions made - Slide 5: Post-operative course and outcomes (blood loss, length of stay, complications) - Slide 6: Pathology result and oncological margin status - Slide 7: Learning points and why this case qualifies for CPD credit - Slide 8: References (EASL HCC Guidelines 2022, ILCA guidelines - cite appropriately) Step 3 - Draft the case report for publication: Draft a Word document titled "Case Report - Laparoscopic Left Lateral Sectionectomy for HCC - Contoso Central Hospital" following CARE case report guidelines: - Title and structured abstract (250 words max) - Introduction: clinical context and what makes this case noteworthy - Patient information: anonymised history, examination, imaging (CT hepatic volumetry) - Diagnostic assessment: AFP levels, BCLC staging, MDT conclusion - Therapeutic intervention: operative technique in full detail (port placement, Pringle manoeuvre if used, transection method, haemostasis approach) - Follow-up and outcomes: 30-day morbidity, LFT trajectory, oncological outcome - Discussion: comparison with open approach; patient selection criteria; learning points for junior surgeons - Conclusion - References (leave as [REF-1], [REF-2] placeholders for me to complete) Flag any sections where clinical detail is missing from the source documents so I know what to fill in manually.

⚠️ Plagiarism and AI disclosure note: If this case report will be submitted to a journal or for CPD credit, do not submit AI-generated text verbatim. Use Copilot's output as a structured first draft only. Rewrite all sections in your own clinical voice, verify every fact and citation against source documents, and comply with the journal's or medical council's AI use disclosure policy before submission.

💡 Also works for: pancreaticogastrostomy, hepaticojejunostomy, robotic distal pancreatectomy, or any technically complex index procedure. Replace the procedure name, case identifier, and relevant surgical team names. Check your medical council's required CPD format before finalising the presentation.

Prompt TipsFor all M365 Copilot surfaces

The GCSE framework: use for every prompt

Goal: What do I want at the end? A dashboard. A 1-page memo. A list of 5 options. Context: Who is it for? What is the situation? What constraints apply? Source: What data am I providing? (Reference the file, paste the data, upload the document) Expectations: Format, length, what to include, what NOT to include. Two lines to add to every prompt: "Do not invent data. Use [PLACEHOLDER] for missing information." "List your assumptions and open questions at the end."

Prompt library: most reusable prompts from today

Market Research Researcher · Critique
Research [topic/company/market]. Compare [3-4 dimensions]. Include recent developments from [timeframe]. Cite all sources. Flag where evidence is limited or conflicting.
Data Analysis Excel Copilot
Analyse [dataset]. Calculate [metric 1], [metric 2]. Compare [dimension A] vs [dimension B]. Identify the top 3 [best/worst performers]. Show as a [chart type] with a summary table. Flag outliers.
Executive Document Word Copilot
Draft a [document type] for [audience]. Context: [situation]. Include: [section 1], [section 2], [section 3]. Tone: [professional/formal/concise]. Maximum [N] words. Do not invent data: use [PLACEHOLDER] for missing figures. End with: assumptions made and open questions.
Agent Test Query Agent Builder
What is the [specific policy / target / guideline] for [specific scenario]? Please cite the section of the document where this is covered.
Delegation Cowork
Cowork: [Describe the desired outcome in plain English]. Then [second task]. Then [third task: can include sending email or scheduling meeting, which requires my approval before executing].
