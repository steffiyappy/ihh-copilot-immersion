About this workshop

A practical, executive-level immersion designed specifically for IHH Healthcare. We move from strategic context → live demos → hands-on exercises, ending with a preview of the Fabric Data Agent for cross-hospital analytics.

What you will leave with

  • Experience using Researcher (Critique + Model Council) for multi-model market intelligence
  • A working ESG dashboard built with Copilot in Excel, and a draft CR report section in Word
  • Your own custom AI agent — Brand Governance, Finance Policy Advisor, or Sustainability Guide
  • A clear mental model of what Copilot can do across your function — and what it cannot
  • An understanding of what the Fabric Data Agent enables for cross-hospital analytics

What this workshop is not

  • It is not a feature walkthrough — every tool is demonstrated in an IHH-specific context
  • It is not about replacing clinical judgment or financial decision-making — Copilot drafts, you decide
  • It is not connected to live IHH systems today — all data used is sample/anonymised

Exercise 1 · Strategic Intelligence15 min · Researcher + Model Council

When to do this: Session 3 starts at 1:30. Exercise 1 runs 1:30–1:45. Open M365 Copilot at copilot.microsoft.com and click Researcher.

Use Researcher's Critique mode (dual-model: generate + review) for deep competitive intelligence on Malaysia's private healthcare market, then use Model Council to see GPT vs Claude side-by-side.

Task 1.15 min🔍 Researcher · Critique mode

Research Malaysia private healthcare market

In M365 Copilot, open Researcher. Keep the model selector on Auto — this activates Critique mode (dual-model: one generates, one reviews sources and evidence grounding). Use this prompt:

Research Malaysia private healthcare market: analyse IHH Healthcare, Columbia Asia, KPJ Healthcare, Sunway Medical. Compare market share, bed capacity, strategic focus, recent M&A activity, digital health initiatives, and medical tourism positioning. Include growth projections for 2026–2028. Cite all sources.
Watch for: Source Reliability indicators and Evidence Grounding scores — these are the Critique model at work, validating every claim in real time.
Task 1.25 min⚖️ Model Council

Side-by-side: GPT vs Claude on strategic opportunities

In Researcher, switch to Model Council in the model picker. Submit this prompt to see GPT and Claude respond simultaneously:

What are the top 3 strategic opportunities for a private hospital group in Malaysia over the next 3 years? Consider: ageing population, medical tourism from Indonesia and the Middle East, digital health penetration, insurance market growth, and the regulatory environment. Provide specific, evidence-based recommendations.
Read the cover letter — it summarises where GPT and Claude agree, where they diverge, and what unique insight each model contributes. This is Model Council's key output.
Task 1.35 min📝 Word Copilot

Turn research into a strategic memo

Open a new Word document. Use Copilot to draft a board-ready memo based on your Researcher output:

Create a 1-page executive memo for the IHH Healthcare Strategy Committee based on the research I just completed in Researcher. Structure: - Market overview: Malaysia private healthcare market size, growth rate, key dynamics - Competitive landscape: IHH's position vs KPJ, Columbia Asia, Sunway Medical - 3 strategic priorities for FY2026–2027 with rationale - 90-day immediate actions (3 bullets) Format: Board-ready, professional, concise. Use [PLACEHOLDER] for any data point not confirmed in the research. Maximum 400 words.

Exercise 2 · ESG/CR Analytics15 min · Excel + Word + Cowork

When to do this: 1:45–2:00. Download IHH_Sustainability_ESG.xlsx and open it in Excel before starting.

Consolidate 12 months of ESG metrics across 6 IHH hospitals, build an executive dashboard, and draft a CR report section — all using Copilot in Excel and Word.

Task 2.15 min📊 Excel Copilot · IHH_Sustainability_ESG.xlsx

Analyse carbon emissions and identify gaps

With the file open in Excel, open the Copilot panel (Home ribbon → Copilot). Run this analysis:

Analyse the ESG data in this file. 1. Which hospital has the highest average carbon emissions? Show the monthly trend for all 6 hospitals. 2. Calculate the year-over-year improvement percentage for each hospital. 3. Compare actual March 2026 figures against the 2026 targets in the Targets_2026 tab. 4. Flag hospitals that are NOT on track for their targets with Red status, on track with Green, and at risk with Amber. Show results as a summary table.
Task 2.25 min📊 Excel Copilot · Dashboard

Build an executive ESG dashboard

Create an ESG dashboard on a new sheet called "Dashboard": 1. A line chart: monthly carbon emissions trend (Apr 2025–Mar 2026) for all 6 hospitals, with a dashed target line for each 2. A bar chart: recycling rate by hospital vs 2026 target — sorted by gap (largest gap first) 3. A RAG status table: each hospital vs each KPI (Carbon, Waste, Energy, Recycling, Renewable) — Red/Amber/Green 4. A callout box: Top 3 hospitals showing the most improvement, and the 1 hospital most at risk Format for C-suite presentation.
Task 2.33 min📝 Word Copilot · CR Report

Draft a CR report section

Open a new Word document. Reference your ESG analysis:

Draft a 2-page Corporate Responsibility report section for IHH Healthcare's FY2025/26 Annual Report. Structure: - Executive summary (3 sentences: headline achievement, key metric, forward commitment) - FY2025/26 Key Achievements: carbon reduction, renewable energy milestones, waste reduction — with specific numbers from the analysis - Sustainability Metrics Table: 6 hospitals vs 2026 targets — RAG status for each KPI - Top 3 Improvement Initiatives (with hospital owner and timeline) - FY2027 Interim Targets aligned with Net Zero 2050 pathway Tone: Professional, factual, suitable for Bursa Malaysia sustainability disclosure and investor relations. Do not invent data — use [PLACEHOLDER] for figures not in the analysis.
Task 2.4 · Bonus2 min👥 Cowork

Delegate the whole workflow to Cowork

Cowork lets you describe an outcome and Copilot orchestrates the steps — with approval checkpoints before it takes actions like sending emails or scheduling meetings.

Cowork: Analyse the ESG data in my recent Excel file (IHH_Sustainability_ESG.xlsx), create a sustainability summary highlighting which hospitals are on track vs at risk for 2026 targets, draft an executive email to the Group Sustainability Committee with the top 3 findings, and schedule a 30-minute review meeting for next week.
Note: Cowork creates a plan first and asks you to approve before taking any action (sending email, scheduling meeting). You stay in control.

Prompt TipsFor all M365 Copilot surfaces

The GCSE framework — use for every prompt

Goal — What do I want at the end? A dashboard. A 1-page memo. A list of 5 options. Context — Who is it for? What is the situation? What constraints apply? Source — What data am I providing? (Reference the file, paste the data, upload the document) Expectations — Format, length, what to include, what NOT to include. Two lines to add to every prompt: "Do not invent data. Use [PLACEHOLDER] for missing information." "List your assumptions and open questions at the end."

Prompt library — most reusable prompts from today

Market Research Researcher · Critique
Research [topic/company/market]. Compare [3-4 dimensions]. Include recent developments from [timeframe]. Cite all sources. Flag where evidence is limited or conflicting.
Data Analysis Excel Copilot
Analyse [dataset]. Calculate [metric 1], [metric 2]. Compare [dimension A] vs [dimension B]. Identify the top 3 [best/worst performers]. Show as a [chart type] with a summary table. Flag outliers.
Executive Document Word Copilot
Draft a [document type] for [audience]. Context: [situation]. Include: [section 1], [section 2], [section 3]. Tone: [professional/formal/concise]. Maximum [N] words. Do not invent data — use [PLACEHOLDER] for missing figures. End with: assumptions made and open questions.
Agent Test Query Agent Builder
What is the [specific policy / target / guideline] for [specific scenario]? Please cite the section of the document where this is covered.
Delegation Cowork
Cowork: [Describe the desired outcome in plain English]. Then [second task]. Then [third task — can include sending email or scheduling meeting, which requires my approval before executing].

Fabric Data AgentSession 4 Preview · Not hands-on today

Session 4 (2:20–2:50) — The presenter will walk through this. Separate step-by-step build guide provided after the session.

What the Fabric Data Agent enables

Connect Copilot Studio to Microsoft Fabric Lakehouse — enabling natural-language queries across all IHH hospitals' operational and financial data in real time.

Sample queries (cross-hospital)

Compare bed occupancy rates across all 6 IHH Malaysia hospitals for the past 3 months. Which is performing below target?
Show me EBITDA margin by hospital for Q1 FY2026. Which hospitals have a variance of more than 5% against budget?
Which hospital has the highest carbon emissions per bed-day? How does this compare to the 2026 target?

Architecture (overview)

  • Data layer: Hospital operational data → Microsoft Fabric Lakehouse (unified data store)
  • Semantic layer: Fabric Data Agent — defines business metrics, KPIs, and relationships
  • Interface: Copilot Studio agent → M365 Copilot chat → natural-language queries
  • Result: Any authorised user can ask cross-hospital questions in plain English, with answers sourced from live Fabric data
Build guide: A full step-by-step Fabric Data Agent build guide (sample data → Fabric → Copilot Studio → agent) will be shared separately after this session.
